Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what Else Pay ("we", "the Service") collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app at app.elsepay.indevs.in you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — first/last name, username, email address, country/city/ZIP, birth date and a hashed password.
  • Device data — device ID, IP address, user agent and approximate location derived from IP.
  • Usage data — ad impressions and completions, check-ins, spins, tasks, referrals, transactions, withdrawals and support tickets.
  • Payment data — the withdrawal/payment method details you choose to provide. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account and keep your balance accurate.
  • To credit rewards and verify them server-side (including AdMob server-side verification callbacks).
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, multiple-account farming and automated traffic.
  • To process withdrawals and provide support.

3. Cookies

We use one first-party, HTTP-only session cookie (else_token) that keeps you signed in. It is Secure, HttpOnly and scoped to .elsepay.indevs.in so it works across the app hosts. Blocking it means you will be logged out on every visit. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share the minimum necessary with:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, TLS and CDN in front of the Service.
  • Google AdMob — to serve rewarded ads and to verify reward callbacks (AdMob receives its own advertising identifiers).
  • Payment / withdrawal providers — only the details needed to pay you.
  • Authorities — where required by law.

5. Retention

Account and transaction records are kept while your account is open and for as long afterwards as needed for accounting, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Usage logs are kept for a limited rolling period.

6. Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Traffic is served over HTTPS only. Access to production data is limited. No system is perfectly secure — if a breach affecting your data occurs we will notify affected users.

7. Your choices

  • You may request a copy of your data, correction, or deletion of your account from the app's support section.
  • Deleting your account removes your profile; anonymised aggregates may remain.
  • You can clear cookies at any time in your browser settings.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and the account will be removed.

9. Third-party links

The app may link to external sites or ads. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app; the date at the top always reflects the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: use Support inside the app, or email the address listed in the app's About section.
