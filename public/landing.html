Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No deposit, no card. You earn by watching ads and completing tasks.

How do I receive money?

Request a withdraw from the wallet page to any enabled payout method. An admin reviews and marks it paid.

Why did my ad earn nothing?

Rewards need a full watch. Closing instantly, emulators and VPNs are blocked by the server's fraud rules.

Can I use two phones?

One account is bound to one device. Lost your phone? Ask support to unlink it.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are salted + scrypt-hashed, sessions are HMAC-signed with 12-hour expiry, and everything runs behind Cloudflare HTTPS. See the privacy policy.
